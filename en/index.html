Admission odds and housing for incoming university students

Problem
Students taking Brazil's national entrance exam (ENEM) pick a program and a city without knowing their real odds of getting in, and then have to find a place to live in a city they don't know.
My role
I created it and build the app, the API and the admin panel on my own.
What I did
I built the app in React Native with Expo for iOS, Android and web. The calculator estimates admission odds from SISU cutoff scores from 2018 to 2025. The housing marketplace has a map, chat and reviews only from people who actually lived there. Behind it sit a Fastify API with PostgreSQL and PostGIS and a React admin panel with role-based access.

About me

Front-End & Mobile with a product focus

I started as an intern in 2021 and have worked on web and mobile interfaces for production products ever since, building fleet tracking, precision agriculture and telehealth systems, plus a social media management platform.

How I work

Habits that keep code standing after the deploy

A test that proves the fix

Every bug fix gets a test that fails without it. Before shipping, I revert the fix and check that the test breaks.

Review and quality

I review and merge teammates' PRs at Kompa and Hype, and keep linting, pre-commit hooks and quality CI on the patient app.

Contract before code

Before touching a payload or an integration, I check the real contract in the backend. At Hype, I documented each social network's rules in the repo.

AI with judgment

I use Claude Code every day, with skills for each repository, a catalog of known pitfalls and a code review before I hand anything off.
