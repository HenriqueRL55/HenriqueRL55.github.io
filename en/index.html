About me

Front-End & Mobile with a product focus

I started as an intern in 2021 and have been building web and mobile interfaces for products in production ever since, from agribusiness to public health. I like interfaces that hold up in the real world: the patient opening a consultation on their phone, the queue that can't freeze, the vehicle that has to be found among 800,000.

Today I build Kompa Saúde's telehealth apps for web, iOS and Android, used by the São Paulo State Health Department, Hospital das Clínicas, the City of Joinville and Unimed cooperatives. Since March 2026 I've also been leading the front end at Hype, a social media management platform for agencies, handling publishing to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and the other networks.
